Findings

Against the conventional sweep, the adaptive schedulers raise reward and the share of transmissions intercepted in the mixed and frequency-agile scenarios. They do not win in the periodic-radar or decoy-heavy scenarios, and they are slower to first contact with brand-new emitters because they spend dwells on bands that already looked active.

The period tracker predicts the next illumination window correctly about half the time or better on periodic radars, but that has not yet translated into higher reward. Every scheduler is far below the oracle: with one narrow receiver, most short transmissions cannot be caught without prior knowledge.
